📍 About Firozpur

Firozpur (also spelled Ferozepur) is a historic city in Punjab, India, located near the Indo-Pak border. Known for its military legacy, cultural richness, and peaceful rural life, it’s a place where tradition and progress blend beautifully.

  • 📅 Founded in the 14th century
  • 🛡️ Hosts one of India's oldest army cantonments
  • 🕌 Rich in Gurudwaras, Temples & Historical Sites
  • 🌾 Surrounded by lush agricultural land and farming communities

🏠 About Virk Farm House

Virk Farm House is our family farmhouse located just outside the city of Firozpur. It reflects our connection to Punjab’s agricultural roots, rural tranquility, and community values. The farm offers open green fields, peaceful views, and a space full of simplicity and warmth.
